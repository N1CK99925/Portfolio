# Surface brief — Home (`src/app/page.tsx`)

## Scope & mode

Single-page portfolio site. Mode: **Experience** — the artifact leads from the first viewport; the interface (the broadcast chrome) recedes into a frame around the work.

## Audience, job, action

- Recruiters / hiring managers skim the cover page and the project index; their action is reaching out.
- Engineering peers and tech leads read the project pages in depth; their action is assessing rigor and, often, recommending.
- The open-to-work signal must be visible without scrolling.

## Proof & content

Real, verifiable facts only: the three projects (LoreSpring, ARES, LMS) with repos and live links, the 96% win-rate claim, the 6-agent claim, contact channels, the name and role. No fabricated metrics, customers, or work history. Resume link points to `/resume.pdf` — a real file the owner must place in `public/`. ARES/LMS screenshots are incumbent remote placeholders; list them for the owner to replace with real screenshots.

## Chosen direction

**The Warm Broadcast** (teletext). The site is a broadcast service (HB-CAST) tuned through pages: a fixed status line (service id, live clock, rolling page number) and a green control row up top; every section is a page (100 Cover, 200 Work, 300 Stack, 400 About, 500 Contact) with double-height saffron headlines, mosaic block graphics, warm-cyan live figures, REVEAL interactions, and HOLD under reduced motion. Warm black ground, cream phosphor, saffron/cyan/green/red broadcast palette.

## Memorable moment

The cover: double-height `HARSHIT BATRA` blockset above a rotating "now showing" work strip, the whole thing framed by the status line's rolling page number — you are not reading a portfolio, you are tuning it in.

## Unresolved decisions

- Resume asset file path/name (owner to provide).
- Real ARES and LMS screenshots (owner to provide).
- Whether the clock in the status line is a live clock or a fixed station time.
